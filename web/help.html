    Use with an AI assistant

    If you work with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, or another), it can keep HelloGitty up to date for you: follow the repositories you start working in, watch the issues you comment on, or turn your project's task list into pipeline stages. Copy this prompt into your assistant, replace the part in square brackets with what you want, and it will propose the changes before making them.

    The prompt names this install's own folder and files, and tells the assistant the ground rules: no tokens in files, no edits to HelloGitty's database or code, and nothing posted to GitHub.
